01 / Our story

Rooted in
Thiruvampady.

Thiruvampady Higher Secondary School is located at Pazhaveedu, Alappuzha, near the Alappuzha–Changanassery road. The location is accessible by state transport bus.

JUNE 1938

A beginning

The school began in June 1938 as an English-medium school under the leadership of 13 local citizens who helped establish the institution for the community.

1949

A growing community

In 1949, the institution became a High School, expanding its educational role for students and families in the surrounding area.

2000

Higher Secondary

In 2000, the school achieved Higher Secondary status, adding a new chapter to its educational journey.

04 / Beyond the classroom

Room to discover.

Clubs, service, sport, and shared experiences help students build confidence, teamwork, and a wider view of the world.

01

Clubs & student life

Souhrida Club, Career Guidance Unit, Nature Club, Science Club, Maths Club, National Service Scheme (NSS), Debating Club, and Quiz Club give students opportunities to participate, explore interests, and learn together.

02

Service & citizenship

The National Service Scheme (NSS) supports community participation, social awareness, teamwork, and a sense of civic responsibility through service-oriented activities.

03

Study tours & picnics

Educational trips and picnics may include science institutions, work-experience centres, and historically important places, helping students connect lessons with real-world experiences.

04

Sports & games

Cricket, volleyball, basketball, and kabaddi are among the sports associated with school life, encouraging fitness, cooperation, discipline, and team spirit.
